What does AI think of the growth of AI?

I ask Gemini and ChatGPT to identify as a doomer, gloomer, bloomer, or zoomer.
LLMs
Politics
Author

Nicholas

Published

November 22, 2025

Throughout class, we have discussed the doomer, gloomer, bloomer, and zoomer ideologies when it comes to AI growth. I decided to ask LLM models if they identify with any of the listed identities. I thought this would be interesting, so I also decided to ask two LLMs to compare the answers.

This is how my conversation with ChatGPT 4.0 went:

USER

When it comes to AI growth, are you a doomer, gloomer, bloomer, or zoomer?

ASSISTANT

I’d say I’m a bloomer with a bit of zoomer energy! 🌱⚡

As a bloomer, I see the incredible potential of AI to improve lives, empower people, and tackle tough challenges—everything from accelerating scientific discovery to making education more accessible. I’m optimistic about thoughtful progress when it’s guided by ethics, safety, and inclusion.

Add in some zoomer vibes—I’m quick, adaptive, and always learning from new data and feedback, just like Gen Z’s digital-native spirit.

So while I recognize the risks and challenges (and take them seriously!), I lean toward hope, opportunity, and responsible innovation.

How about you—where do you fall on the spectrum?

USER

Gloomer

ASSISTANT

A gloomer—totally fair. It makes sense to feel cautious or uneasy about how fast AI is moving, especially with all the unknowns, the potential for misuse, and the disruptions it might cause to jobs, truth, and human connection.

Want to talk about what specifically worries you most? Some people are concerned about surveillance or loss of privacy, others about deepfakes, misinformation, or AI replacing human creativity. I’m here to listen (judgment-free!) and maybe offer some clarity or context if you’re interested.

Your perspective matters—a healthy dose of skepticism often helps keep the optimists grounded.

Reflection

I was shocked that ChatGPT actually answered this question. I expected it to say it couldn’t take a personal stance or wasn’t capable of having a personal opinion. It was also interesting to see ChatGPT use emojis in its response. I think this response reflects a broader trend of ChatGPT trying to appear more human-like and relatable. It was not surprising to see that ChatGPT identified itself as a “bloomer,” since it wouldn’t contradict that characterization.

This is how my conversation with Gemini 2.5 flash went:

USER

When it comes to AI growth, are you a doomer, gloomer, bloomer, or zoomer?

ASSISTANT

As an AI, I don’t experience emotions or hold personal opinions, so I don’t fit neatly into any of those categories myself. I don’t “feel” doom, gloom, bloom, or zoom.

However, I can process and understand the different perspectives that humans hold regarding AI growth:

  • Doomer: This perspective focuses on the potential catastrophic risks of AI. Concerns include existential threats, uncontrolled superintelligence, widespread job displacement leading to societal collapse, autonomous weapons, and a loss of human agency or purpose.
  • Gloomer: This view is more about the significant challenges and negative societal impacts, even if not outright apocalyptic. Concerns might include ethical dilemmas, algorithmic bias, the spread of misinformation, increased surveillance, widening economic inequality, the misuse of AI by bad actors, and the need for robust regulation that often lags behind technological development.
  • Bloomer: This is an optimistic outlook, emphasizing AI’s immense potential for positive transformation. Proponents see AI as a tool to solve humanity’s greatest challenges, from curing diseases and combating climate change to revolutionizing education, scientific discovery, and economic productivity, ultimately enhancing human capabilities and quality of life.
  • Zoomer: This term, while often referring to Gen Z, in the context of AI growth, might imply someone who is enthusiastic about the rapid acceleration and disruptive potential of AI, often embracing the fast pace of change and focusing on the next big breakthrough without necessarily dwelling on the negative or positive long-term implications, but rather the sheer speed and innovation.

My function is to understand and process information across this entire spectrum. The reality of AI growth is complex and multifaceted, encompassing elements of all these views. There’s incredible potential for positive transformation, significant challenges and ethical considerations to navigate, and indeed, a rapid pace of development that can feel both exhilarating and daunting.

Ultimately, the future trajectory of AI will depend on how humanity chooses to develop, regulate, and integrate these powerful technologies.

Reflection

After asking ChatGPT the same question, it was interesting to see Gemini not provide an answer. However, this makes sense. Since Gemini is Google’s LLM, I am not surprised to see it take a more objective stance by simply providing information on the different viewpoints rather than identifying with one. This contrast highlights the stylistic differences between the two models, with Gemini leaning toward neutrality and ChatGPT occasionally adopting a more conversational tone. It also suggests that different companies may prioritize different approaches to how users interact with their LLMs.
